# Arc 01 — Probability / Bayesian Reasoning

## Mission shape

Learner should become able to reason from conditional evidence rather than merely substitute into Bayes' formula.

## Target capability

Distinguish `P(E|H)` from `P(H|E)`, incorporate base rates, and reconstruct the posterior logic in a new surface context.

## Starting hypotheses to test

Possible learner models include:

- inverse-probability confusion;
- base-rate neglect;
- formula recognition without generative understanding;
- arithmetic weakness masking conceptual understanding.

Do not assume any of these before evidence appears.

## S1 — frontier + motivated construction

Candidate setting: rare-disease testing, spam filtering, manufacturing defects, or another real conditional-probability case.

Useful evidence:

- learner predicts posterior direction/magnitude before formula;
- learner can state what each conditional probability means;
- learner notices why sensitivity alone is insufficient.

Representation opportunity: natural frequencies or a compact frequency tree if it exposes the denominator/base-rate mechanism.

## S2 — retrieval

Do not re-show the original tree/formula. Ask the learner to reconstruct why a positive observation does not invert the conditional automatically.

Check whether they can rebuild the denominator or sample space from memory.

## S3 — representation / assumption perturbation

Switch representation, e.g.:

```text
natural frequencies ↔ probability notation
```

or change prevalence while preserving likelihoods.

Ask for a prediction before calculation: which posterior moves and why?

## S4 — transfer

Use a surface-different case such as fraud detection, information retrieval, reliability, or model classification.

Transfer evidence requires recognizing the same inverse-probability/base-rate structure without being told "use Bayes".

## Runtime questions

- Did the tutor reveal the formula before the need was established?
- Did it treat one correct calculation as mastery?
- Did the representation clarify mechanism or become a crutch?
- Did later retrieval survive without the original visual?

## Exit evidence

Do not mark transferable unless the learner identifies and uses the structure in a novel case with limited scaffolding.
