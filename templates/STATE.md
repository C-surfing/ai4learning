## State-transition discipline

- `exposed`: learner has encountered the idea; no independent-use claim.
- `developing`: partial independent use or explanation exists, but evidence is incomplete or scaffold-dependent.
- `stable`: at least two meaningfully independent pieces of evidence support reliable use; immediate imitation alone is insufficient.
- `transferable`: learner succeeds in a meaningfully novel context with limited scaffolding.

Treat these as conservative defaults, not mechanical counters. Record why an exception is justified.
